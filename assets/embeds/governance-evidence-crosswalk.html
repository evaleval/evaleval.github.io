Governance evidence across evaluation schemas

Each governance requirement is split into the specific pieces of evidence a record would need to hold. Each band links one of those pieces to a field that could record it, in Every Eval Ever, AutoBenchmarkCards, Evaluation Cards or AEF-1. Switch schemas on and off to see which gaps each one closes. Pink bands are evidence that no selected schema can hold.
